Music analysis · 2026

Sibelius: Symphony No. 1, Op. 39

A beat-by-beat harmonic study of the introduction and exposition of the first movement of Jean Sibelius's Symphony No. 1 in E minor. The full score was transcribed from the 1902 Breitkopf & Härtel edition and every sounding pitch of the eighteen staves read quarter-note beat by beat; the readings are organised into themes, paragraphs and phrases, with the key chord progression of each phrase reduced to a grand staff.

Movement I, bars 1–175 (introduction and exposition) available

Korngold: String Quartet No. 2, Op. 26

A beat-by-beat harmonic study of Erich Wolfgang Korngold's String Quartet No. 2 in E♭ major. The score was transcribed from the IMSLP edition and read quarter-note beat by beat; the readings are organised into themes, paragraphs and phrases, with the key chord progression of each phrase engraved on a grand staff.
