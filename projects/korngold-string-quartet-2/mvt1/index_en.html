E♭ major is the home key. The movement is read as an exposition (mm.0–47), a development (m.48–77 beat 1&), a recapitulation (m.77 beat 3+2/3–115) and a coda (mm.116–137). Theme 2 and the closing material, set in a B♭ framework in the exposition, return in the recapitulation measure for measure with mm.22–47, a perfect fifth lower by pitch class, in E♭. The development’s F♯7 block and its Tranquillo transformation of Theme 1 in G♭ are reread in the home key in the coda, and the movement ends with the opening B♭–C–B♭ motto stated three times in augmentation and a ♭II–I close without a dominant (mm.135–137).
